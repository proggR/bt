Social Capital Is Axiomatic

For a decade and a half I've been driven by notions of socialcapital/socialcapitalism.

Examples:

  • sourced.fm is an abstract model of bottom-up socialcapitalism
  • Githubberment captures a process of consensus development
  • Social Insurance 2.0 captures an ontology for bottom-up society
  • Fractional proposes a more tunable model for a fiat currency
  • Commonwealth combines them, democratizing money via distributed identities, meritocratic processes and priority of the commons.
